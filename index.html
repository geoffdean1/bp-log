Events on the chart

Mark medication changes, illness, travel etc. They appear as numbered flags on the chart and on the report.

Import

CSV or pasted Excel rows. Columns: Date then either Sys, Dia, Pulse (one reading per row) or Sys1, Dia1, Sys2, Dia2, Sys3, Dia3. A header row is detected automatically. Dates like 3/14/2025 or 2025-03-14 both work.

Export & backup

Your data lives only in this browser on this device — it is never uploaded anywhere. Back up now and then, especially before changing phones.
